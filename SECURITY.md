# Reporting a security issue

If you find a security problem on zanmode.com or anything else ZanMode runs, please tell us privately first.

## How to report

Write to support@zanmode.com with "Security" in the subject. Include:

- What you found and where.
- Steps to reproduce it.
- What someone could do with it.
- How to reach you for questions.

Don't open a public issue or post it in Discord.

## What we ask

- Only use your own account and your own builds.
- Don't access, change or delete other people's information.
- Don't do anything that slows down or breaks the site for others.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix it before you talk about it in public.

## What you can expect

We reply within three working days, keep you posted while we fix it, and credit you when it's fixed if you'd like that.
