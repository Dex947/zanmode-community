# Recognition

This page explains how we credit people's work here and how the leaderboard, seasons, hall of fame and roles work.

## Credit comes first

- Accepted work carries your name in the Lab so people see who made it when they pick it. Every phone will have a credits page listing the people whose work is inside it.
- Every accepted piece is posted in #hall-of-fame on Discord along with the people or team who made it.
- Everyone's first submission gets a reply within two days. If the review takes longer, we'll at least tell you we've seen it.

## Seasons and the leaderboard

A season is a calendar quarter: January to March, April to June, July to September, October to December. The [leaderboard](LEADERBOARD.md) updates every Monday, and the numbers behind it are in [leaderboard/](leaderboard/).

Points only count once a maintainer or juror has labelled the work. Nothing counts just for posting.

| What | Points |
|---|---|
| Accepted work, small / medium / large | 5 / 15 / 40 |
| Challenge entry on the shortlist | +10 |
| Challenge winner | +25 |
| Your first accepted work ever | +5 |
| Review on someone else's pull request that gets merged | 2, up to 10 a week |
| Answer marked as the answer in Discussions | 2, up to 10 a week |
| Bug or test report we confirm | 3 |
| Helping a newcomer to their first accepted work | 5 |
| Running a meetup, call or event | 5 |

There are three kinds of board. One is overall, one is per studio and one is for people in their first season so newcomers have a board they can place on.

When a season ends:

- The top three in each studio get the Season Champion role for the next season.
- Their names go in the [hall of fame](HALL_OF_FAME.md) for good.
- Points start again from zero. The hall of fame keeps the podiums and everyone's count of accepted work.

People with the same points share the place. When a prize has a cut-off, the person with more accepted pieces goes first, and if it's still a tie the jurors decide.

## Keeping it fair

- Only maintainers and jurors can add the labels that score.
- Reviewing your own work doesn't count.
- Reviews and answers are capped each week.
- A maintainer can mark any item `no-points`.
- Jurors don't score on briefs they judge.
- The numbers are public. If something looks wrong, open an issue before the season closes and we'll fix it.
- Nobody moves up the ladder on points alone. The leaderboard shows who to look at and the studio lead makes the call.
- We don't count messages, commits, stars or invites.

## Maker of the month

Anyone can [nominate someone](../../issues/new?template=nomination.yml) and add a line on what they made or did. The jurors pick one person each month. They get the Maker of the Month role for that month and a write-up in #hall-of-fame.

## Founding members

Everyone who joins before ZanMode launches gets the Founding Member role and keeps it.

## Seats

Some roles are limited on purpose.

- Each studio has up to two Lead seats. A seat lasts a year and can be renewed.
- Maintainers look after an area. If a maintainer is away for six months, they become emeritus. Their name stays in the credits and their review rights pause until they ask to pick the area back up.
- Regulars and up get the #regulars-lounge. The team shares early looks there and asks for opinions first.

## What happens when

- Every week: a co-working session in the Workshop voice room and a post crediting everything accepted that week.
- Every month: a new challenge, demo day on the Demo Day stage, maker of the month and office hours in each studio.
- Every quarter: the season closes and the new champions are named.
- Every year: a community day with local meetups.

## Meetups

Pick your region when you join Discord and you get its channel. Plan a meetup, a call or a build session in #meetups. Use one post per meetup and tag it with your region. People who run events get the Host role, and each event earns 5 points once you [tell us how it went](../../issues/new?template=event.yml).
