# Test studio

For testers, bug hunters and security researchers.

## What you can do now

- Try the Lab on different phones, tablets and computers, and report what breaks or confuses you.
- Try new features before they go live, and tell us what works.
- Report security issues privately, following [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## How to report a bug

Open a [site bug](../../../issues/new?template=site-bug.yml). Say what you did, what you expected, what happened, and your device and browser. A screenshot or a short screen recording helps a lot.

## What's next

Early software on real phones and longer checks of battery, heat and drops once there are units to test.
