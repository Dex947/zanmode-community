# Hardware studio

For electronics, mechanical, thermal and radio engineers, and repairers.

## What you can make now

- Cases, docks, stands, chargers and other accessories.
- Tools that make repair easier.
- Fixtures and tools for checking that things fit and work.

## What we give you

- Part sizes and space around them for any build in the Lab.
- CAD and electronics templates for accessories.
- The outside shape of the body for case and dock makers once a phone exists.

Ask in #hardware-studio for the latest files.

## How work is judged

Each brief states what counts as done. Anything physical is checked by our team on real hardware before it's accepted. Safety is never put to a vote. Batteries, charging, radio and heat are always approved by the ZanMode team.

## What's next

Paid briefs for harder work. Later on, contributors who've built up a track record here can work on parts that go inside the phone.
