# Stories studio

For video creators, writers, translators and hosts.

## What the studio makes

- Guides on building a phone for gaming, photos, battery life or a small hand.
- Build videos and reviews of what people design in the Lab.
- Translations of the site into your language.
- Events: build-alongs, design reviews, calls with the team.

## What we give you

- The ZanMode logos and brand colours.
- Early looks at new features to cover.
- Help from the team when you need a fact checked.

Ask in #stories-studio for the brand files.

## Rules for content

- Say clearly when something is your opinion.
- Don't present prices from the Lab as final. They're estimates and the site says so.
- If there's ever sponsored content, mark it as sponsored.

## What's next

Launch films made with the community and a translator for every language people ask for.
