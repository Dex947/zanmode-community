# Briefs

A brief is a piece of work we want done. Open briefs are the [issues labelled brief](../../../issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Abrief).

## What every brief says

- The problem: what people need and why.
- The limits: sizes, power, heat and anything else the work has to fit.
- The bar: what counts as done and how it's checked.
- Who can take it: open to everyone, or to contributors in a studio.
- What you get: credit, a prize, a payment, or a mix.
- The deadline, if there is one.

## Three kinds

| Kind | What it is | How it's judged |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge | Many people enter, a few are picked | A jury scores every entry against the criteria in the brief. People who use the Lab also vote, counting as one juror |
| Bounty | One person or team does it, for a set payment | It meets the bar in the brief |
| Project | Bigger work in stages | Each stage is agreed up front and reviewed when it's done |

Challenges pay everyone on the shortlist, not only the winner.

## How to take a brief

1. Comment on the issue to say you're on it, or that you're entering.
2. Ask questions in the issue or in your studio's channel.
3. Submit the way the brief asks, before the deadline.
4. The studio reviews it and replies on the issue.

## Suggesting a brief

If you think something needs doing, open an [idea](../../../issues/new?template=idea.yml). Studio leads turn good ideas into briefs.

## Challenge rules

Every challenge has its own rules, linked from its brief, and they win over anything here. Entering is always free. Winners are picked on skill against the written criteria, never by chance.
