# Code of conduct

We want the ZanMode community to be a place where anyone who cares about phones can learn, make things and be taken seriously. That needs a few rules everyone keeps.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. When you disagree, talk about the idea and leave the person out of it.
- Give credit. Name the people whose work you build on.
- Give useful feedback. Say what works, what doesn't, and why.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Keep private things private, yours and other people's.

## What isn't allowed

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Hate or discrimination of any kind, including about gender, sexuality, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, nationality or appearance.
- Sexual content or unwanted sexual attention.
- Spam, scams, unsolicited ads or self-promotion that has nothing to do with the conversation.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Passing off other people's work as yours or posting work you have no right to share.
- Posting confidential material from an employer, a client or anyone else.
- Pretending to speak for ZanMode or to be on the team when you aren't.

## Where it applies

On the ZanMode Discord, in the ZanMode GitHub repositories and discussions, at our events, and anywhere you represent the community.

## Age

You need to be at least 16 to join. Paid briefs also need you to be old enough to sign a contract where you live.

## Reporting

If something here makes you or someone else unsafe or unwelcome, tell us:

- Message a moderator on Discord or use the report button on the message.
- Or write to support@zanmode.com with "Conduct" in the subject.

Reports are read by the moderators and kept private. If a report is about a moderator, that person isn't involved in handling it.

## What happens next

Moderators look at what happened and who was affected and then choose a response. Usually it's one of these in order, but serious harm can go straight to a ban:

1. Correction: a private note about what went wrong. A public apology may be asked for.
2. Warning: a formal warning and a time-out from the conversation it came from.
3. Temporary ban: no contact with the community for a set time.
4. Permanent ban: removal from every ZanMode community space.

The goal is to stop the harm and repair it where we can. If you think a decision was wrong, write to support@zanmode.com and someone who wasn't involved will look at it again.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and by Mozilla's community participation guidelines.
