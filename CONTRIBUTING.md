# How to contribute

Thanks for wanting to help. Here's how to take part.

## Start here

1. Join the [Discord](https://zanmode.com/discord) and pick your studios when it asks. You'll get the channels for them.
2. Say hello in #introductions: what you do and what you'd like to make.
3. Briefs aren't open yet. When they are, they'll be announced in [Announcements](../../discussions/categories/announcements). Until then, share what you've made in [Show and tell](../../discussions/categories/show-and-tell) and post ideas in [Ideas](../../discussions/categories/ideas).

## Ways to help

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Answer a brief | Comment on its issue to say you're on it, then send a [submission](../../issues/new?template=submission.yml) |
| Suggest something | Open an [idea](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml) or start a thread in Ideas |
| Report a problem on zanmode.com | Open a [site bug](../../issues/new?template=site-bug.yml) |
| Report a security issue | Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Don't post it in public |
| Improve these docs | Open a pull request |
| Show what you made | Post it in Show and tell or in your studio's channel |
| Run a meetup or call | Plan it in #meetups on Discord, then tell us [how it went](../../issues/new?template=event.yml) |
| Thank someone | [Nominate them](../../issues/new?template=nomination.yml) for maker of the month |

## Submitting work

Every brief says what to send. In general:

- Design: images at full size (PNG or SVG), the source file (Figma, Blender, Illustrator or similar), and a short note on what it is and how it's meant to be used.
- Hardware: sketches or renders, CAD in STEP, and a note on materials, sizes and how it's put together.
- Software: a link to the code or a pull request, how to run it, and screenshots.
- Stories: a link to the video, guide or translation, and the language and audience it's for.

What we need with every submission:

- It's your own work or you have the right to use everything in it.
- You list any fonts, models, code, music or photos you used that aren't yours along with their licences.
- Nothing confidential from an employer or anyone else.

## Ownership

Your work stays yours while it's being reviewed. If it's accepted, you license it to ZanMode on the terms written in that brief, and your name goes on it. If it isn't accepted, it stays yours and nobody owes anything either way.

## Pull requests to this repository

Sign off your commits with `git commit -s`. The sign-off says you wrote the change or have the right to submit it under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org). Keep changes small and say why you made them.

## What happens after you submit

Someone from the studio replies within two days. Reviews can take longer, but you won't be left without an answer. If it's accepted, a maintainer labels it, it's posted in #hall-of-fame, and it counts for the season leaderboard.

## Credit and rewards

Accepted work carries your name in the Lab. Some briefs pay, and some are challenges with prizes for the shortlist. The brief says which before you start. Nothing is paid for work that wasn't asked for in a brief. [Recognition](RECOGNITION.md) covers points, seasons and roles.

## Questions

Ask in Discord or in [Q&A](../../discussions/categories/q-a). For anything private, write to support@zanmode.com.
