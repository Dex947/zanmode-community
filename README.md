# ZanMode community

ZanMode is a phone you design yourself part by part. In the Lab at [zanmode.com](https://zanmode.com) you pick the screen, chip, cameras, battery, cooling and body, see it in 3D, and see what every choice changes. Launching soon.

This is where designers, electronics engineers, software developers, testers, writers and creators meet to make those phones better.

New here? Read [Start here](../../discussions/8).

## Where things stand

The community has just opened, so for now it's about meeting each other and getting set up. No briefs are open yet and there's nothing to work on. When the first briefs open they'll be posted in [Announcements](../../discussions/categories/announcements) and on Discord.

What you can do today:

1. Join the [Discord](https://zanmode.com/discord) and pick your studios and region.
2. Say hello in [Introduce yourself](../../discussions/7).
3. Design a phone in the [Lab](https://zanmode.com/build) and share it in [Show and tell](../../discussions/categories/show-and-tell).
4. Ask anything in [Q&A](../../discussions/categories/q-a) or post an idea in [Ideas](../../discussions/categories/ideas).

## How work will flow

1. People design phones in the Lab and save them.
2. We post briefs: a look, an accessory, a feature, a translation, a guide.
3. You answer a brief, or bring your own idea.
4. The studio reviews it. Anything that goes inside a phone is also checked by our team.
5. Accepted work shows up in the Lab for people to pick, and your name goes on it.

## Meet people near you

Discord has a channel for each region and a #meetups forum for planning meetups, calls and build sessions. The Workshop voice room is open any time if you'd like company while you work.

## Credit

What you make carries your name. When people pick your look, part or preset in the Lab, they see who made it. Every ZanMode phone will have a credits page listing the people whose work is inside it.

Some briefs come with a prize or payment. Each one says so up front and comes with its own rules.
