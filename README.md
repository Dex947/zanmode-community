# ZanMode community

ZanMode is a phone you design yourself, part by part. In the Lab at [zanmode.com](https://zanmode.com) you pick the screen, chip, cameras, battery, cooling and body, see it in 3D, and see what every choice changes. Launching soon.

This is where the people who make those phones better meet: designers, electronics engineers, software developers, testers, writers and creators.

## How it works

1. People design phones in the Lab and save them.
2. We post briefs: a look, an accessory, a feature, a translation, a guide.
3. You answer a brief, or bring your own idea.
4. The studio reviews it. Anything that goes inside a phone is also checked by our team.
5. Accepted work shows up where people can pick it in the Lab, with your name on it.

## Studios

You can join more than one.

| Studio | Who it's for | What you can make now |
|---|---|---|
| [Design](studios/design.md) | Industrial, colour, visual, motion and sound designers, 3D artists | Ready-made looks, backs and camera islands, startups, lock screens, sounds, 3D models of parts |
| [Hardware](studios/hardware.md) | Electronics, mechanical, thermal and radio engineers, repairers | Cases, docks, chargers and other accessories, tools for repair and checking |
| [Software](studios/software.md) | App, system and tools developers, accessibility specialists | Themes, lock screens, tools, accessibility reviews of the Lab |
| [Test](studios/test.md) | Testers, bug hunters, security researchers | Bug reports on the Lab, reviews of new features, security reports |
| [Stories](studios/stories.md) | Video creators, writers, translators, hosts | Guides, build videos, translations of the site, events |

## Join

- Discord: [Join the server](https://zanmode.com/discord) for talking, studio channels and events. When you join, pick your studios and your region.
- [Discussions](../../discussions) for ideas, questions and showing your work.
- [Briefs](../../issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Abrief) are the open tasks. Each one says what it needs, how it's judged and what you get.

## Get recognised

Accepted work earns points for the season [leaderboard](LEADERBOARD.md), and each season's top three in every studio go in the [hall of fame](HALL_OF_FAME.md). There's also a maker of the month, roles you can grow into and a lounge for regulars. [Recognition](RECOGNITION.md) has the details, including what counts and how we keep it fair.

## Meet people near you

Discord has a channel for each region and a #meetups forum for planning meetups, calls and build sessions. The Workshop voice room is open any time if you'd like company while you work.

New here? Read [how to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Credit

What you make carries your name. When people pick your look, part or preset in the Lab, they see who made it. Every ZanMode phone will have a credits page listing the people whose work is inside it.

Some briefs come with a prize or payment. Each one says so up front, with its own rules.
